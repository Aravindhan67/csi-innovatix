Pixel Perfect: The CSS Art Battle

A fast-paced creative showdown where teams race to recreate complex images using only HTML & CSS.

About the Event

"Pixel Perfect" is a 60-minute creative showdown where teams race to recreate a secret, complex image—such as a famous painting, landmark, or brand logo—using only HTML and CSS, with absolutely no images, SVGs, or external assets allowed.

Competition Rules

  • Planning (5 mins): Teams have a short period to strategize their approach after the target image is revealed.
  • Execution (45 mins): Craft your visual replica entirely from <div> elements and styles.
  • Techniques: Leverage gradients, shadows, clipping, and positioning to match the target as closely as possible.

Judging Quality

Winners are judged on visual accuracy, CSS craftsmanship, and the creativity of their code structure, pushing the limits of what can be built with pure styling.

Event Info

  • Duration 60 Minutes
  • Team Size Individual or 2
  • Category Creative Coding
